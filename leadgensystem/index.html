What you'd actually be buying

Not leads. The thing that makes them.

Lead vendors run outbound texting to homeowner lists, keep the replies, and sell them on per lead. We build that same machine inside a CRM set up for your business, and an AI works it around the clock.

The leads it produces are yours and nobody else's. They aren't sold on to three other roofers in your market, which is the part pay-per-lead can't offer.

How it gets built

Three pieces, all handled by us.

01
Carrier registration (A2P)
Your business numbers get registered and verified with the carriers so your texts actually land instead of failing silently. This is the step most contractors skip, and it's why their outbound quietly stops working. Takes the carriers 3 to 10 days. We handle all of it.
02
Homeowner data
We pull a targeted list in your service area — filtered by location, property type and ownership tenure — scrubbed and skip-traced to mobile numbers. Your first 20,000 records are included free in month one. Additional records after that are about a penny each.
03
The texting system, and the AI that runs it
Outbound campaigns built inside your CRM at about a penny per text, no cap on volume. An AI answers every homeowner who writes back, in seconds, at any hour, and picks up when they call. It asks what's going on with the roof and sorts the serious from the curious.

Before the call

Questions that come up a lot.

How is this different from the lead companies I already use?
They keep the system and sell you the output, often selling the same homeowner to several contractors in the same market. Here the system runs for you and only you. Your leads go to nobody else, and every conversation sits in your own pipeline rather than a vendor's.
Do you book the appointments for me?
No. The AI works the conversation and qualifies the homeowner, then drops them into your pipeline. Your team calls them and books their own appointments. That's deliberate — the booking conversation is where your crew's availability and judgement matter.
How soon does it start producing?
Texting doesn't begin the day you sign. Carrier registration takes 3 to 10 days, the build takes one to two weeks, and new numbers ramp their daily volume gradually so the carriers trust them. In the account above, the first lead landed three days after the first text went out — but that first text is a few weeks after day one.
What kind of volume should I plan on?
We plan conservatively: roughly one positive lead per 600 texts sent. On that basis 10,000 texts is about 17 leads, 25,000 is about 42, and 50,000 is about 83. The live account above did considerably better than that — we'd rather set the floor low and beat it. Results depend on your market, your list and your follow-up.
What do I have to do?
Seven pieces of paperwork at the start — business name, EIN, address, phone, email, website, and the zips you want worked. After that, call the people who raise their hand. That's the whole job on your end.
