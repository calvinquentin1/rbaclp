1 · The usual agency deal

You've probably been pitched this before.

  • A big retainer up front, before you've seen a single lead
  • A long contract, so you're locked in whether it works or not
  • Setup months where you pay and wait
  • Leads that may be sold to other companies in your area too

All of the risk sits with you. If it doesn't work, you find out after the money's gone.

2 · How we like to work

You should see it work before you commit to anything big.

So we start small. Before we talk about anything bigger, we put a couple of exclusive homeowner leads in front of your company and let them speak for themselves.

If the leads turn into jobs, we talk about what's next. If they don't, no hard feelings. Either way, you decide with real numbers in front of you, not a sales pitch.

3 · From an ad to your phone

Start to finish, here's how a lead reaches you.

  1. 1 · Ads

    We run the ads

    Facebook and Instagram ads in the cities you serve, offering homeowners help with their heating and cooling. They run under our homeowner brand, Cosa Home Services.

  2. 2 · Request

    A homeowner asks for help

    They answer a few quick questions on a page made for your company.

  3. 3 · Delivery

    The lead hits your phone

    You get a text and an email with their details the moment they submit.

  4. 4 · Your team

    You call and win the job

    Call while they're still thinking about it, set the visit, quote the work.

It takes days, not hours, to get going. We'll give you the timing for your area on the call.

4 · What the homeowner fills out

A few quick questions, and your company's name on it.

  1. What do you need help with?
  2. Do you own the home?
  3. What's the address?
  4. Name, mobile phone and email

Your company is named in the consent box, so the homeowner agrees to hear from you. Renters are screened out before a request counts as a lead.

5 · Exclusive means exclusive

Every lead goes to a single company.

We never share a lead, and we never resell it. You're not racing other HVAC companies to the same homeowner, and you're not competing on price before you've even called.

6 · What we need from you

About 10 minutes of info.

  • Your company's legal name (it goes in the homeowner's consent box)
  • The cities you serve
  • The HVAC services you want more of
  • The phone and email where leads should go

We'll walk through the rest on the call. Nothing to prepare before then.

7 · After we start

You've seen the service. Now you decide what's next.

  1. Option 1

    Keep going

    If it's working, we keep the leads coming and look at the numbers together.

  2. Option 2

    Build something bigger

    If you want more volume, we talk about a longer-term program built around your area and your team.

  3. Option 3

    Walk away

    No hard feelings.

We'll look at your real numbers together before you pick.

8 · What we'll cover on the call

15 to 20 minutes, all about your business.

  • Your service area and the jobs you want more of
  • How you handle new leads today, and who calls them
  • How we would start in your area, and how fast it can happen
  • Your questions
