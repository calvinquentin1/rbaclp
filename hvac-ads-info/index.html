5 · What happens when a lead comes in

From the ad to a booked inspection, often before you've seen the lead.

This is the part most ad agencies skip. Fast, cheap Facebook forms can bring in people who tapped by accident, so every lead gets checked and answered right away, while they're still thinking about their system.

  1. The ad

    A homeowner in your area sees your ad

    Your company name, your real job photos, and a free inspection.

  2. The form

    They answer a quick question or two

    Do they own the home, and what do they need. The form makes them review their answers before they send, which cuts the accidental ones.

  3. The calendar

    They pick a time for the inspection

    Right after the form, they can book straight onto your calendar, before they've even closed Facebook.

  4. Within 60 sec

    A text from your company

    Booked? "You're booked for Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. We'll reach out before then for a few details." Not booked? "We got your request. Pick a time here, or someone from our team will reach out shortly."

  5. Right away

    Your salesperson's phone buzzes

    Name, phone number, what they need and where they are. Not booked yet? They call while the homeowner is still looking at their phone. If nobody has called in 15 minutes, you get an alert.

  6. 7 days

    Follow-up until they answer

    Anyone who doesn't book gets a friendly text and a call from your team over the week, each text with a link to book. It stops the moment they reply or book.

  7. Every week

    Your snapshot

    Leads, how fast each one was answered, and estimates booked. Booked and sold jobs also go back to Meta, so it finds more homeowners like them.

11 · Questions owners ask

The short answers.

We tried Facebook ads. The leads were junk.

Usually the ad talked to anyone, the form asked nothing, and nobody called back for hours. We fix all three: ads aimed at homeowners in your area, a form that asks if they own the home and what they need, and a text within 60 seconds.

Are the leads shared?

No. They come from ads in your company's name, to your form. Nobody else gets them.

Isn't Google better?

For someone whose AC died at 11 PM, Google search is strong. Facebook reaches homeowners before they search: the ones with an aging system who are thinking about replacing it. Many companies run both. We focus on Facebook and Instagram, and on answering every lead fast.

How fast will leads come in?

Your ads go live within 14 days of getting access and photos. Leads can start in the first few days, and the cost per lead usually settles as Meta learns who responds.

Do I need a CRM?

No. If you already have one that texts (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge and others), we plug your leads straight into it. If you don't, we set one up for you at no extra cost, with a local texting number registered to your business. Either way, your salesperson gets a text on the phone they already carry.

Am I locked in?

The first 2 months, then month to month. If you leave, you keep the ad account, the ads and every lead.

What if it doesn't work?

We look at it in closed jobs, not lead counts. For our first clients this season, the second half of the setup isn't due until the ads bring you your first closed job. Every lead is in your lead list, so you can check every name.
